Where We Deliver

We deliver to all 64 districts of Bangladesh through our courier partners.

Delivery Time

  • Inside Dhaka: usually 1–2 working days.
  • Outside Dhaka: usually 3–5 working days.

These are estimates from when your order is handed to the courier. Holidays, weather and courier delays can occasionally make delivery take longer.

Delivery Charge

A delivery charge applies based on whether your address is inside or outside Dhaka. The exact charge is shown at checkout before you confirm your order. When a free-delivery offer is running, its minimum order amount is shown on the site and the discount is applied automatically at checkout. Some products and bundles also qualify for free delivery, which is shown on the product.

Order Confirmation and Tracking

After you place an order you will see a confirmation page, and we will email you if you gave us an email address. You can follow your order in My Orders or on our order tracking page using your order number and phone number.

Receiving Your Order

  • Please make sure someone is available at the delivery address and that your phone is reachable, as the courier may call you.
  • For cash-on-delivery orders, please have the order total ready.
  • Check the parcel when it arrives. If an item is wrong or damaged, contact us within 48 hours with a photo (see our Return Policy).

Cancellation After Dispatch

Once your order is handed to the courier it can no longer be cancelled. You can refuse a cash-on-delivery parcel at the door, or request a return after delivery.
