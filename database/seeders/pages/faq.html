Orders & Payments

How do I place an order?

Browse our catalogue, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Fill in your delivery details, choose a payment method and confirm your order. You will see an order confirmation page straight away, and we will email you a confirmation if you gave us an email address.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) across Bangladesh, and bKash and Nagad. For bKash or Nagad, send the order total to the number shown at checkout and enter your Transaction ID; we confirm your order once the payment is matched. We will never ask for your PIN.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, until your order is handed to our courier partner. If you have an account, go to My Orders and cancel it there. If you checked out as a guest, contact us as soon as possible. Once the courier has your order it can no longer be cancelled, but you can request a return after delivery.

Is there a limit on how many I can order?

Cash-on-delivery orders are limited to 10 units of the same product per phone number until earlier orders are dispatched. For larger quantities, pay by bKash or Nagad, or contact us.

Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Delivery within Dhaka usually takes 1–2 working days. Deliveries to other districts usually take 3–5 working days. Holidays, weather and courier delays can occasionally make it longer.

Is there a delivery charge?

Yes, a delivery charge applies based on whether you are inside or outside Dhaka. The exact charge is shown at checkout before you confirm. When a free-delivery offer is running, its minimum order amount is shown on the site and the discount is applied automatically at checkout.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes, we deliver to all 64 districts across Bangladesh through our courier partners. See our Shipping Policy for details.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery for items that are unused, undamaged and in their original packaging. If you have an account, open the order in My Orders and choose Request a return; guests can contact us. See our Return Policy.

How long does a refund take?

Once we receive and check the returned item, we refund the product price to your bKash or Nagad number within 3–5 working days. The delivery charge is not refunded. See our Refund Policy.

What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?

We're sorry. Please contact us within 48 hours of receiving your order with a photo of the item. We will send a replacement at no extra cost, or refund the product price. We cover the cost of returning a wrong or damaged item.

Account & Support

Do I need an account to shop?

If guest checkout is available, you can order without an account. An account lets you track and cancel orders, request returns, save addresses and manage your wishlist.

How can I track my order?

Log in and visit My Orders, or use the order tracking page with your order number and phone number.

How do I contact customer support?

You can reach us through our contact page, by phone or by email. Our support hours are shown at the bottom of every page and on the contact page.
