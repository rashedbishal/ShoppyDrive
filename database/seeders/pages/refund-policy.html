When You Get a Refund

We refund you when a return is approved (see our Return Policy), when you choose a refund instead of a replacement for a wrong or damaged item, or when we cancel an order you have already paid for.

How Much Is Refunded

  • We refund the product price you actually paid, after any coupon or discount.
  • The delivery charge is not refunded.
  • If you only return some items from an order, the refund covers those items.
  • Loyalty points you used or earned on a refunded order may be adjusted to match.

How and When You Receive It

We send refunds to your bKash or Nagad number within 3–5 working days after we receive and check the returned item (or after we cancel a paid order). Please make sure we have the correct number; contact us if it has changed.

Cash-on-Delivery Orders

If you refused a cash-on-delivery parcel or the order was cancelled before delivery, you have not paid anything, so there is nothing to refund.

Questions

Contact us through our contact page with your order number. The seller's details are shown at the bottom of every page.
