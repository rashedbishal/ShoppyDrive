Return Window

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery. After 7 days, returns can no longer be requested.

Condition of Returned Items

Items must be unused, undamaged, and returned in their original packaging with any tags and accessories included. We check every returned item when it reaches us, and we may refuse a return that does not meet these conditions.

How to Request a Return

  • With an account: go to My Orders, open the delivered order and choose Request a return. Tell us which items and why.
  • Guest orders: contact us through our contact page with your order number and phone number.

We will review your request and let you know whether it is approved and how to send the item back.

Return Delivery Costs

  • Wrong or damaged item: we pay the cost of returning it. Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item.
  • Change of mind: you pay the delivery cost of sending the item back to us.

Wrong or Damaged Items

If you receive a wrong or damaged item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement at no extra cost, or refund the product price under our Refund Policy.

Refusing a Cash-on-Delivery Parcel

An order that is already with our courier cannot be cancelled. If you no longer want a cash-on-delivery order, you may refuse it at the door; you have not paid for it, so there is nothing to refund.
