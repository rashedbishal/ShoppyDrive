Introduction

Welcome to ShoppyDrive ("ShoppyDrive", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, make a purchase, or sign in using a third-party account such as Google or Facebook. This policy applies to all visitors and customers. The seller responsible for your data is shown at the bottom of every page.

Information You Give Us

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, place an order, or contact us for support. This includes:

  • Name, email address, and password
  • Delivery address
  • Phone number
  • For bKash or Nagad payments, the Transaction ID you enter and the payment details in the confirmation message we receive. We never ask for or store your PIN, and we do not accept or store card details.
  • Order history, reviews, returns and preferences

If you choose to create an account or sign in using Sign in with Google or Continue with Facebook, we receive only the basic profile information those providers release to us with your consent, typically your name, email address, and public profile photo. We never receive or have access to your Google or Facebook password.

Information We Collect Automatically

  • With every order: the IP address and browser/device information (user agent) used to place it, and how you reached our site (for example, a search engine, social media or an ad). We use this to prevent fraud and fake orders and to understand which channels bring customers.
  • Unfinished checkouts: if you enter your phone number at checkout but do not complete the order, we save the phone number, name, email and cart items you entered. We use this to help you finish your order, and, if you have an account, we may send you one reminder email with an unsubscribe link. This record is deleted when you place an order with that phone number.
  • Visits: we count daily visits using a small cookie, separately from any optional analytics.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and authenticate your account, including sign-in via Google or Facebook
  • Process, deliver and support your orders, returns and refunds
  • Send order confirmations and order updates by email
  • Prevent fraud, fake orders and abuse of our website
  • Respond to your comments and questions
  • Send promotional communications (only if you opt in)
  • Improve our website and services

Signing In With Google or Facebook

ShoppyDrive offers "Sign in with Google" and "Continue with Facebook" as optional, faster ways to create an account and log in. When you use either option:

  • We request only the minimum data needed to identify you and create your account: your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • This data is used solely to create or match your ShoppyDrive account, authenticate you on future visits, and pre-fill your profile. It is never used to post on your behalf, access your contacts, or sold to advertisers.
  • ShoppyDrive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • ShoppyDrive's use of information received from Facebook Login complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Facebook's Data Policy.
  • You can unlink your Google or Facebook account from ShoppyDrive, or revoke ShoppyDrive's access entirely from your Google or Facebook account security settings, at any time.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We share only what is needed with trusted service providers who help us run our business: our courier partners receive your name, phone number and delivery address to deliver your order, and our email provider sends our emails. They may use this information only to provide their service to us.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and passwords are stored using one-way hashing.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your cart and preferences, count visits, remember how you reached our site, and, if you allow it, analyse site traffic. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent, but some parts of the site, such as checkout, may not work without them.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep order, payment, return and refund records for as long as we need them for accounting, customer support, fraud prevention and our legal obligations, even after an account is deleted.

Your Rights

You have the right to access and correct your personal data, and to ask us to delete it. To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address listed on our contact page.

Deleting Your Account

  • Self-service: While signed in, go to Settings → Profile → Delete Account.
  • By request: Email us at support@shoppydrive.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" from the email address on your account, or reach us via our contact page. We will verify your request and act on it within 30 days.

If you have never placed an order, your account and its personal data are deleted. If you have orders with us, we remove your name, phone number, saved addresses and any Google or Facebook link from your account, but we keep your email address and your order records (including the delivery details on those orders) for the reasons above. Deleting your account always removes the link between your ShoppyDrive profile and your Google or Facebook account.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our contact page or email support@shoppydrive.com.
